# Separate owned summon healing

Status: accepted · 2026-10-18

Healing summons can contribute friendly healing while dealing zero damage.
Generic pet damage/DPS tables do not fully describe their recorded actions.
The existing combat pet entries now retain optional `healing_support`, with
friendly healing, credited absorption and a whole-fight rate. Shared saved
`healing_context.owned_summon_examples` exposes the same individual evidence in
Report and Playbook. No class-specific renderer, schema migration or ranking
policy is introduced.

Master Pet ownership, selected-fight friendlyPets identity/game ID/owner and
exported source ownership must agree with the selected Player. Trusted outgoing
pet coverage and exact unrestricted encounter event-set proof gate complete
amounts. Possible raw or normalized support rows are validated over the full
fight before exclusions; only consistent typed unrelated events may be skipped.
Projection occurrence multiplicity must match, preventing duplicate ability
metadata from multiplying event sums. Recipient classification reuses the shared
healing helper after these integrity checks.

Unknown attribution stays unknown. An individually verified pet can retain its
measurement beside an incomplete overall listing, but no complete all-summon
total follows. Actor support, cohort scores and recorded WCL HPS remain exact.
Names, damage absence or approximate score agreement never establish ownership,
assignment, quality or a ranked-healing policy.

Initial activation uses current Era MC/BWL/AQ40 scope in the shared generic class
adapter. Dedicated Hunter/Druid analysis and historical Naxx behavior remain
unchanged. Renderers read optional saved fields only; older saved documents do
not acquire new observations during rendering.
