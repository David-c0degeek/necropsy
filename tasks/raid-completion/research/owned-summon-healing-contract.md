## Failure reconstruction and guardrails

PM-1: a healer sees zero-damage pets and cannot find their contribution. Extend
the existing generic combat pet entries and shared saved healing context; show
friendly healing/credited absorption per known owned unit on both pages, with
whole-fight scope and explicit assignment/ranking limits. No separate Shaman
renderer or parallel pipeline. Preserve actor metrics and original WCL scores.

PM-2: another owner's totem is credited, a malformed row disappears before
validation, or unknown ownership becomes a complete zero. `owned_pets` only
selects exported stream ownership, so verify exact unique owner Player and pet
Pet identities against master and selected-fight friendlyPets bindings. Reject
conflicting game IDs/owner IDs or duplicate identities. An incomplete overall
listing does not invalidate independently bound individual units, but never
certifies an all-summon aggregate. No ownership inference from HPS fit, names,
spells or timing. Avaztwo remains unknown and unassigned.

For the new support fields validate possible raw OR normalized outgoing
heal/absorbed candidates over the full selected fight before source/time/type
exclusions. Require literal integer raw identity/time/fight/spell bindings,
agreement with normalized values, finite nonnegative amounts and explicit
recipient identity. Reuse recipient classification only after this guard.
Trusted pet SOURCE coverage and exact encounter All/event-set proof establish
the outgoing measurement footprint; TARGET is not an outgoing-healing proof.
Unknown, boss and conflicting recipients cannot become friendly support.

PM-3: analyzer-generated expected values mask double counting, or adding fields
changes old saved/Naxx artifacts. Freeze independent raw pet healing/absorption,
retain every previous register expectation, and reuse the shared max-multiplicity
event set. Initially gate the new optional fields to current Era raid scope via
`duty.applies`; preserve Hunter and Druid engines and legacy documents. Render
saved fields only, never recompute from the database during rendering.
