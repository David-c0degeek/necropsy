# AQ40 ranking-source boundary

Reviewed 2026-10-17. No ranking policy is activated by this research note.

The WCL/Archon
[Fresh AQ parsing article](https://www.archon.gg/classic-fresh/articles/news/fresh-ahn-qiraj-progress-parsing-and-speedrun-rules),
updated September2025, is scoped to Fresh Anniversary, with a separately
qualified Hardcore Fresh statement. It discusses controlled-player damage,
Royalty healing resets/adds, PI credit and Life Tap restrictions. These are
investigation prompts, not Era partition1/2 authority. Do not import its rules
into zone2005 merely because the bosses share names.

[Wipefest's July2025 AQ announcement](https://www.archon.gg/classic-fresh/articles/news/wipefest-temple-of-ahnqiraj-aq-40)
demonstrates an existing Fresh analysis service. Its assignment-sensitive
examples reinforce checking duties before interpreting incoming damage. It
does not establish this repository's Era target eligibility, phase boundaries
or ranking arithmetic, and no service or plugin was installed.

Current native captures explicitly request API`dps`/`hps`; the separate
`bossdps` enum and website defaults cannot redefine them. Verify exact raw API
amount rows, selected actor/pet ownership, target identity and recorded fight
duration. Skeram images, Royalty order, Fankriss spawns, Viscidus participation,
Twin schools and Ouro re-emergence require distinct evidence. Preserve residuals
and unknowns rather than fitting exclusions to a single parse.

The expanded healer arithmetic investigation independently resolves native
API amount rows for322 retained records/173 distinct actor-fights. Rates match
six-significant-digit rounding across the sampled Priest Holy and Druid
Balance/HPS sources. This is empirical precision evidence, not a documented
universal serialization/credit policy. Low HPS needs a narrower interval than
a blanket0.005; actual pet/guardian, hostile/control-time and other-class policy
coverage remains open. See
[exact residuals and source member hashes](healing-score-precision-investigation.json).
