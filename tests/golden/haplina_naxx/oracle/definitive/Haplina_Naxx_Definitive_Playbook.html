Global setup

  • Spec: 20/31/0 once live hit is solved. This remains the dominant parse build in the downloaded top-log dataset.
  • World buff: DMF Damage, not DMF Agility, on serious parse attempts.
  • Core ranged gear: Nerubian Slavemaker + Sniper Scope remains the overwhelmingly dominant ranged setup in the downloaded top-five dataset.
  • Armor pattern: 7-piece Cryptstalker with the PvP Multi-Shot gloves is the dominant endgame pattern in the dataset.
  • Pet: Use a high-DPS physical pet profile and manage it manually. The downloaded top logs overwhelmingly use Claw-based pets; Haplina's pet uptime/output remains one of the largest controllable gaps.
  • Ammo: Doomshot for serious attempts.
  • Engineering: Pre-use Battle Chicken / Arcanite Dragonling where appropriate and fit Dense Dynamite only into truly empty ranged windows.
  • Renataki: High-value boss-specific tool. Do NOT treat it as a universal or Patchwerk-mandatory trinket; the Alliance Patchwerk examples inside the current overall top five do not use it.
  • Faction rule: Haplina is Alliance. Use Alliance entries inside the current overall top five as the primary execution benchmark whenever Horde racial/Windfury effects materially change the comparison.

Corrections that matter after faction normalization

  • Patchwerk rank 1 Smcadogx is Horde, proven Orc by Blood Fury, with actual Windfury extra-attack events. His full melee package is a Horde ceiling example, not Haplina's direct benchmark.
  • Alliance Patchwerk weaving is still real: Charies, Andrenn and Xal use 9 / 8 / 6 Raptor Strikes with zero detected Windfury extra-attack events and essentially no white-melee package.
  • Xert is the clean Alliance pure-ranged Patchwerk reference: 1347 DPS, 0 Raptor Strikes.
  • Grobbulus weaving is removed from the Haplina plan: the meaningful current top-five weave is the Orc + Windfury example; the Alliance entries stay ranged.
  • Thaddius heavy weaving is downgraded to opportunistic-only for Alliance. The Alliance leaders show small Raptor inserts, not the Horde heavy-weave pattern.
  • Renataki remains excellent on several bosses, but the faction-corrected Patchwerk recommendation is Slayer + Mark first; Blackhand + Mark is already proven in the current top three.

Truth model / what every label means

What is directly observed

  • Overall current top-five ranking rows pulled from WCL for each boss.
  • Gear, spec where CombatantInfo exposes it, player/pet event streams, Raptor/white-melee counts, and detected Windfury extra-attack events.
  • Faction from the WCL combatant/report context.
  • Race only when a race-defining racial cast is actually observed, e.g. Blood Fury → Orc or Berserking → Troll.

What is deliberately NOT overclaimed

  • “Alliance comparator” means the best Alliance entry inside the downloaded current overall top five. We did not separately query a top-five-Alliance-only leaderboard.
  • “WF procs = 0” means no Windfury extra-attack events were detected for that Hunter. It is not automatically proof that a totem was never present in the group.
  • Pet family is labeled “likely” when inferred from ability compatibility rather than explicitly named by WCL.
  • Shot-count targets are comparison benchmarks, not claims that mechanics always allow every projected shot.
